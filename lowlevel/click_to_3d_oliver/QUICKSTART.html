Click-to-3D: Quick Start

Click a chess piece on screen and the SO-101 arm picks it up and puts it on another square.

⚠ Safety: three keys move the real arm
  • k pick and place: after about 35 s of planning, the arm moves straight away, with no further prompt.
  • a moves to a click (asks for Enter in the terminal first); w drives to the viewing pose with no prompt.
  • h releases the arm (torque off): hold it first, or it drops. Keep the area around the arm clear. To stop a move, switch off the arm's power: keys are ignored while it moves.

The window: left = wrist camera (on the gripper), middle = tripod camera, right = status panel. Some moves ask questions in the terminal instead: if the window says "keys ignored until done", look there.

Steps

  1. Start the app (power on the arm and plug in both cameras first) 
    cd ~/OXAI_hardware_lab/LeRobot_OXAI/lowlevel/click_to_3d_oliver
~/venvs/click3d/bin/python run_click_to_3d.py --robot --fk-repose
    Wrong way round (tripod on the left)? Press x to swap the cameras.
  2. Put the arm in the viewing pose: press w
    • The arm drives to a saved pose where the wrist camera sees the board. It moves immediately.
    • No pose saved yet? Hold the arm where the wrist camera sees most of the board, press h to hold it, then e to save that pose.
  3. Calibrate the cameras to the board: press c
    • Type a square both cameras can see in full (e.g. d 4) and press Enter. It doesn't need a piece on it.
    • Click that square's 4 corners on the wrist view (left), then again on the tripod view (middle).

    Corner 1 (green) is the corner nearest a1. Then go counter-clockwise as it looks on screen: 2 = toward the h-file, 3 = the far corner, 4 = toward rank 8.
    Clicks snap to the detected corners. Hold Shift while clicking to place one exactly where you click.

    Check it worked: a cyan grid is drawn over the tripod view. It must sit on the real squares, with a1 and h8 labels in the right corners. If not, press c again.
  4. Click the piece to move
    • Press space, then click where the piece touches the board (its base, not its top) on the wrist view, then press y (on the board).
    • Or click it on the tripod view instead (no y needed).
    • An orange 1 marks it on both views.
  5. Click the destination square: space, click anywhere on that square, then y. A white 2 marks it. The panel shows "piece 1 (c6) → square 2 (c4 centre)": check those squares are right.
  6. Pick and place: press k ⚠
    • It plans in simulation (about 35 s; watch the terminal), then the arm moves on its own: picks piece 1, places it on the centre of square 2, and returns.
    • If the plan fails, nothing moves and the terminal says why.
  7. Next move: press w to go back to the viewing pose, then repeat from step 4.
    Redo step 3 (c) only if the board, the robot or the tripod has moved, or the grid stops lining up.

Keys

Calibrate & view
cCalibrate both cameras to the board (step 3)
lGrid overlay on the tripod view on / off: check it sits on the real squares
xSwap the cameras (wrist must be on the left)
fCorner clicks: snap to detected corners / exactly where you click
v3D view of board, cameras and last click, with the click's distance from the square centre
rLive preview of both cameras
Click points
spaceStart a click (then click the wrist or tripod view)
y / nAfter a wrist click: the point is on the board / above it (n: then click the same point on the line drawn in the tripod view)
gGold-mask: outline a piece with SAM on both views to find its grasp point (advanced)
Move the arm
kPick the piece at click 1, place it on click 2's square. Moves without asking after planning
aMove the gripper tip to the last click (confirm with Enter in the terminal)
wDrive to the saved viewing pose. Moves without asking
hHold the arm (torque on) / release it (torque off: support it first)
eSave the arm's current pose as the viewing pose (twice within 3 s to replace one)
Settings (current value shown in the panel)
oJoint compensation for a / k: off → baseline → on
bWhere board positions come from: tape-measured board (ruler) / camera-detected (cv)
mLens / URDF / mount file menu
uWrist camera mount calibration (separate window; see README)
qQuit

If something goes wrong

You seeDo this
Grid doesn't sit on the squares, or a1 / h8 in the wrong cornerPress c again. The tripod may have moved, or the saved calibration is old.
"pose rejected" after the 4 cornersWrong corner order. Start at the corner nearest a1, go counter-clockwise on screen, and check you typed the right square.
Rings don't snap to a cornerHold Shift while clicking, or press f for exact clicks.
"keys ignored until done" / window frozenThe terminal is waiting for an answer. Look there.
"hit outside board"The click missed the board. Click again, on the board.
"RAY GAP … mm"The wrist and tripod clicks don't meet: click the same point in both, or redo c.
Yellow BACKUP IN USE bannerNormal for the tripod camera. For the wrist camera it means its lens file is missing: ask a teammate.
Arm goes somewhere unexpectedSwitch off the arm's power. Keys are ignored while it moves, so h won't stop it.
