# October: e-file grasp offset and place offset (16–24 Oct)

Early work on one move at a time (e4→e5, e3→e5, e2→e5), before the 3×3 neighbourhood and block experiments.
No separate notes were kept at the time; this page is rebuilt from each run's saved config and results.

**How to read the figure names**: `<date>_<time>_<from>_to_<to>__landings__<robot_knows_start | robot_blind>__<starts>__grasp_<x>_<y>_<z>__place_<x>_<y>_<z>[__<variant>]` (offsets in mm).
- *robot_knows_start*: the rook starts off-centre and the robot is told the true offset (tests the move itself).
- *robot_blind*: the rook starts off-centre (σ = 3.3 mm) and the robot aims at the square centre (tests tolerance to position error).
- *grasp_offset_sweep*: landing accuracy for a grid of grasp offsets around the given centre (blind, 30 samples each).
- *variants*: `default` / `e3best` = grasp offset used; `grip-*` = gripper physics tweaks (pinch softening off, hold forces, jaw/piece friction, lower by 20).

Scored on landing accuracy (this was before the pickup-only focus). Raw per-sample data: `../_archive/` (see `../README_VINCENT.md`).
